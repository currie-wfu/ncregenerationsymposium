About the meeting

Regeneration in focus

The NC Regeneration Biology Symposium brings together labs across North Carolina studying regeneration, tissue repair, cell and developmental biology, and biomedical engineering.

From salamander limbs and zebrafish fins to engineered stem-cell niches and bioprinted tissues, the meeting highlights the robust regenerative biology research happening across North Carolina.

Registration is free! Registration and abstract deadlines, program details, and travel logistics coming soon!
